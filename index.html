A library for people who collect the pieces that were never money.

Tokens, medals and so-called dollars have their own mints, their own catalogs and their own societies. This page keeps the good ones in one place.

A marketplace for buying and selling is on the way. The library comes first.

The library

Mints and makers

  • Moonlight Mint

    Daniel Carr's private mint in Colorado. Medals, tokens and fantasy pieces struck on his own presses, with open-house dates posted on the site.

    moonlightmint.com

References and catalogs

  • So-Called Dollars

    The online companion to Hibler and Kappen's reference, kept by the Coin & Currency Institute. Look up HK numbers, varieties and pieces the book never listed.

    so-calleddollars.com
  • Newman Numismatic Portal

    Washington University's free digital library: auction catalogs, periodicals and decades of the TAMS Journal, all searchable.

    nnp.wustl.edu
  • Numista

    A collector-built catalog of coins, tokens and medals from every country, with photographs and collection tracking.

    numista.com

Societies

  • Token and Medal Society

    The hobby's society since 1960. Publishes the TAMS Journal and keeps the maverick database for tokens nobody has attributed yet.

    tokenandmedal.org

Marketplace

Tokens and Medals will become a place to buy and sell with other collectors. Until it opens, the library above is the whole site. Know a resource that belongs here? Send it along.
