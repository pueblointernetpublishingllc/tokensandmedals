About Tokens and Medals

Tokens and Medals is a reference guide for collectors of tokens, medals and so-called dollars. It is published by Pueblo Internet Publishing LLC in Pueblo, Colorado.

What is here

  • The library: links to catalogs, societies, archives and makers, each with a short description of what it covers.
  • The decoder: type a catalog number and see which reference it comes from and where to look it up.
  • The price guide: our estimates of value for slabbed so-called dollars and for Daniel Carr's Moonlight Mint pieces, produced by a statistical formula. They are opinions, not appraisals or offers.
  • The Colorado pages: articles on Lesher Referendum dollars, Pueblo tokens and scrip, and the state's private mints, with sources listed at the foot of each page.

How the site is run

  • Nothing here is sponsored, and no link is an affiliate link. A link to a site is not an endorsement of anything it sells.
  • The library descriptions are written by us to say what each resource covers.
  • The price guide publishes estimates only. The sales it was built from are not reproduced.
  • The Daniel Carr price guide reads live gold and silver prices when the page opens. Your browser asks a public Ethereum node (publicnode.com) for the PAXG trading price on Uniswap and Chainlink's price feeds, and Hyperliquid's public API (api.hyperliquid.xyz) for the silver perpetual's price; nothing else is sent.
  • AI assistance was used in researching, writing and building this site. Mistakes are possible.
