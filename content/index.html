About this site

Tokens and Medals is a reading list for exonumia: the tokens, medals, so-called dollars and other struck pieces that circulated beside money without ever being money. The hobby's best references are scattered across private mints, society journals, university libraries and collector-built catalogs. This site keeps the ones worth knowing in one place.

Every entry is a site we use ourselves. Nothing here is sponsored, and no link is an endorsement of anything a site sells. Descriptions are ours, written to say what each resource is good for. The Colorado pages are our own research, with sources listed at the foot of each page; corrections are welcome.

The site is published by Pueblo Internet Publishing LLC in Pueblo, Colorado, and it will grow as the list does.
